Services

One service. Your whole textile, handled.

We cover the full lifecycle of professional textiles —from supply to traceability— and tailor it to the level your operation needs. Here is what we do in each service and what we manage for you.

01

Textile rental

Access high-end textiles without buying them or tying up capital. We supply, maintain and renew; you pay for use.

What we do

  • Supply of hotel- and healthcare-grade professional textiles
  • Maintenance, laundering and replacement included in a pay-per-use fee
  • Automatic renewal of items at the end of their service life
  • Stock scaled to season and occupancy, with no one-off purchases

What we manage

  • Bed linen
  • Towels & terry
  • Bathrobes & slippers
  • Table linen
  • Uniforms
02

Industrial laundry

Controlled processes in our own plants, with hygiene and finishing standards worthy of five-star and clinical environments.

What we do

  • Scheduled collection and sorting by client and item type
  • Washing and disinfection with hygiene controls (RABC / EN 14065 reference)
  • Drying, pressing and professional finishing, item by item
  • Quality control and removal of defective items before delivery

What we manage

  • Flatwork
  • Terry & towelling
  • Table linen
  • Uniforms
  • Healthcare textiles
03

Replacement & logistics

Textiles arrive when they should, in the quantity they should, without you having to ask. Routes built around your operation.

What we do

  • Scheduled collection and delivery routes tailored to you
  • Safety stock to cover occupancy peaks and events
  • On-time deliveries with agreed service levels (SLA)
  • Management of linen rooms and points of use on your premises

What we manage

  • Scheduled deliveries
  • Safety stock
  • Linen-room management
  • Nationwide coverage
04

Personalization

Your identity, woven into every detail. We make textiles speak your brand's language.

What we do

  • Embroidery, screen printing and labelling with your corporate identity
  • Bespoke pieces in colour, fabric and weight
  • Integrated RFID labelling on every item for traceability
  • Distinct lines by property, plant or category

What we manage

  • Logo embroidery
  • Corporate trims & colour
  • Woven labels
  • Bespoke pieces
05

Traceability & RFID management

Every item identified and tracked in real time: you know what you have, where it is and its condition. Full control, zero invisible loss.

What we do

  • Per-item identification with laundry-proof RFID chips
  • Automatic counting on collection and delivery, no manual counts
  • Real-time inventory and service-life tracking of every item
  • Consumption, loss and replacement reports for your team

What we manage

  • RFID chip per item
  • Real-time inventory
  • Consumption reports
  • Loss control
