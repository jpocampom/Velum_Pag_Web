Hospitality

Hospitality

For 4- and 5-star hotels, resorts and spas where the feel of a sheet is part of the experience. Whiteness, softness and availability, night after night.

What we do

  • End-to-end management of bed and bath linen with consistent quality
  • Spa and pool textiles with agile replacement in high season
  • Table linen for the hotel's F&B, ready for every service
  • Frequencies and par stock matched to real occupancy

What we manage

  • Percale & sateen sheets
  • Duvet covers & nordic duvets
  • Pillows & mattress protectors
  • Terry towels & bathrobes
  • Slippers & shower curtains
  • Bedspreads, bed runners & plaids

Healthcare

Healthcare

For hospitals, clinics and care homes where hygiene allows no nuance. Validated processes, per-item traceability and absolute reliability.

What we do

  • Validated wash processes with biocontamination control (RABC)
  • Per-item traceability and documentation for audits
  • Management of patient linen, uniforms and technical textiles
  • Reliable replacement: care wards cannot run out of textiles

What we manage

  • Clinical bed linen
  • Patient gowns & pyjamas
  • Healthcare uniforms
  • Reusable surgical textiles
  • Microfibre & technical cloths
