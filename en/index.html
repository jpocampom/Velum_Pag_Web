Frequently asked questions

What you'll want to know.

What is textile rental and how does it work?
It's the rental of bed linen, table linen and other textiles with laundering, maintenance and replenishment included for a recurring fee. You don't buy textiles or tie up capital: you pay per use and we take care of everything else.
What are the advantages of outsourcing a hotel's or restaurant's laundry?
You eliminate investment in machinery and staff, turn a fixed cost into a variable one based on occupancy, free up space and secure constant quality and availability. Your team focuses on the guest, not on the textiles.
How much does the service cost?
It depends on volume, type of item, occupancy and frequency. That's why we always start with a free textile assessment: we analyse your operation and present a tailored proposal, with no obligation.
Is rental more sustainable than buying and washing?
Yes. The professional management model extends the working life of every item, optimises water and energy at industrial scale and reduces textile waste through repair, reuse and responsible recycling.
Do you comply with hygiene regulations for healthcare textiles?
We work to standard UNE-EN 14065 (biocontamination control, RABC) and to ISO quality standards, with documented traceability. The hygiene of healthcare textiles is an absolute, documented priority.
Which areas do you serve?
We provide coverage across Spain with our own plants and a network of approved partners, and soon in Portugal. Wherever you are, the standard of service is the same.
How do you manage replenishment and textile losses?
We control your inventory and replenish according to your occupancy, minimising losses: you always know what you have and where it is.
