What's worth knowing.

Is VELUM a laundry?
No. VELUM is a premium textile care platform built on a renting model. Caring for the textiles is part of the service, but what we deliver is availability: the right textiles, always ready, without you having to own or manage them.
What does «renting-first» mean?
It means you don't buy textiles or tie up capital in them. We supply, maintain and replenish them; you use them and pay to have them always available. Maintenance is the support, not the headline.
Do you work across all of Spain?
Yes. The standard is born in our two owned plants and extends through a nationwide network of approved partners. The result is the same anywhere in the country. Portugal is coming soon.
Can I choose the fabric and the finish?
Entirely. We don't work from a fixed catalogue: we choose fabric, weight and finish to suit your brand and your category. It's 100% tailored.
What happens if I need more textiles in peak season?
We plan for it with you. We adjust your live stock to your peaks so you never run short and never have too much. Predictability, with no nasty surprises.
How do I know what textiles I have at any moment?
With traced inventory and consumption in plain sight. You always know what you have, where it is and what condition it's in, with no surprises and no discrepancies.
Who looks after me when something comes up?
A single point of contact who knows your business and answers for the entire service. No chains of suppliers and no half-answers.
