Room

Rest, cared for down to the detail.

We dress the bed from basic to fully bespoke, with fabrics that improve wash after wash.

Sheets and cases

Percale and sateen, from blends to 100% cotton, in thread counts from 250 to 1,000 — Egyptian cotton included. Made-to-measure hems and a colour range (white, ivory, beige, grey and blue).

Duvet and pillow covers

Bag, bottle or flange finishes; sizes from single to King, in the same qualities as the sheet line.

Protectors and toppers

Waterproof, breathable protectors; siliconised-fibre toppers (1,100 g/m²) or feather-down (1,900 g/m²) for high-end comfort.

Bedspreads, plaids and bed runners

Piqué and ottoman from 195 to 285 g/m²; polar and raschel blankets and plaids up to 600 g/m², in a wide colour range.

Bath

High-end touch and absorbency.

The full terry range, from light and fluffy to maximum weight, always with minimal shrinkage.

Towels by use

Face, hand, shower, bath and bath-sheet in 100% cotton American terry, 420 to 650 g/m², plain or with a border.

Bathrobes

American terry, velour-sheared, waffle or velour, 250 to 550 g/m²; shawl or kimono collar, sizes up to XXXL.

Bath mats

High-weight terry, 650 to 850 g/m², plain or with a decorative border.

Pool and spa

Solid, high-fastness colour towels (450–600 g/m²) for gym, spa, sauna and pool.

Dining

The table, dressed to your standard.

Highly durable table linen, crease-resistant and soft to the touch, fit for industrial laundering.

Tablecloths and overlays

100% cotton or blend sateen, coloured weave and linen; Soft System finish, in white and a wide colour range, with coordinated drops for any table.

Napkins and table runners

Same fabric and finish as the cloth to match; standard 55×55 cm napkin and made-to-measure runners.

Kitchen linen

Cloths and towels in cotton and blends fit for industrial laundering, with hemstitch or scallop finishes.

Healthcare

Hygiene and resistance, no compromise.

Healthcare textiles for demanding hygiene protocols and RABC industrial laundering, without sacrificing patient comfort.

Clinical bed linen

Sheets, cases and draw sheets in percale and sateen approved for high-temperature laundering (UNE-EN ISO 15797).

Sanitary mattress protection

Microfibre or Tencel protectors with a polyurethane barrier: waterproof, breathable and washable up to 95 °C.

Patient gowns and uniforms

Garments and uniforms in fabrics approved for thermal disinfection; identification by colour, size and labelling for traceability.

Care homes and surgery

Absorbent underpads and protectors, bibs for geriatric care and surgical drapes (open or closed), in fabrics approved for high-temperature laundering.
