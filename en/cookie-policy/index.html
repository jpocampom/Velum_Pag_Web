1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on the user's device while browsing. They serve to enable the site to function, to remember preferences and, where applicable, to obtain statistical information. The term also includes other similar technologies (pixels, web beacons, local storage).

2. Types of cookies

  • Technical or necessary: they enable browsing and the use of basic functionalities (security, remembering the state of the cookie banner). They do not require consent.
  • Preferences or personalisation: they remember information such as the language (ES/EN/PT). They require consent.
  • Analytics or measurement: they make it possible to analyse the use of the Website statistically in order to improve it. They require consent.
  • Marketing or behavioural advertising: they manage advertising spaces according to the browsing profile. They require consent.

4. Consent

In accordance with article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE and the AEPD Guidelines on the use of cookies, non-technical cookies (analytics, preferences and marketing) are not installed until the user gives their consent through the banner. The user can accept all, reject all or configure them granularly. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.

5. How to manage or withdraw consent

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from the «Cookie settings» link available in the site footer. You can also manage or delete cookies from your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari). Disabling technical cookies may affect the operation of the Website.
