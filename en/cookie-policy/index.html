1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on the user's device (computer, tablet or mobile phone) while browsing. They enable the site to function properly, remember preferences and, where the user consents, obtain statistical information about its use.

In this policy, the term "cookies" is used broadly and also includes other similar technologies that allow information to be stored and retrieved on the device, such as the browser's local storage (localStorage), pixels or web beacons. On this Website, the user's consent preference is in fact stored using the browser's local storage, and not as an HTTP cookie (see section 3).

3. Which cookies does this website use?

The Website is static and trilingual (Spanish, English and Portuguese). It only uses necessary technical cookies and technologies and, subject to the user's consent, analytics cookies. No marketing or behavioural advertising cookies are used.

3.1. Technical cookies and storage (necessary — no consent required)

These are essential for the operation of the Website and for storing the user's decision regarding cookies. They are used regardless of consent, in accordance with article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE.

3.2. Analytics cookies (consent required)

These make it possible to measure the use of the Website on an aggregated and statistical basis (pages visited, language, device) in order to improve it. Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Ltd., is used with the IP address anonymised and under Google Consent Mode v2. 4. Legal basis

  • Technical or necessary cookies: legitimate interest in the proper functioning of the Website and the exception in article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE, which does not require consent for strictly necessary cookies.
  • Analytics cookies: the user's consent, in accordance with article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE and Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). This consent is freely given, informed, specific and unambiguous, and may be withdrawn at any time.

5. Consent mechanism

When first accessing the Website, a cookie banner appears with three options presented with equal availability and prominence: "Accept all", "Reject all" and "Configure".

  • The "Configure" option opens a preferences panel organised by category, with a toggle for analytics cookies. Necessary cookies are always active and cannot be disabled.
  • By default no analytics cookie is installed: thanks to Google Consent Mode v2, all non-necessary categories remain denied until the user accepts them. Google Analytics 4 is only loaded if the user consents to the analytics category.
  • The user's preference is stored in the browser's local storage under the key velum_cookie_consent_v2.

6. International data transfers

If the user consents to analytics cookies, Google may process certain data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), in particular in the United States. Such transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and, on a supplementary basis, by the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) approved by the European Commission, together with any applicable additional safeguards. You can find further information in Google's privacy policy.

7. Managing and withdrawing consent

You may change or withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it:

  • From the Website: via the "Cookie settings" link available in the footer, which reopens the preferences panel.
  • From your browser: you can allow, block or delete the cookies installed on your device through your browser settings:
    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Apple Safari
    • Microsoft Edge

8. Consent retention period

The consent preference is retained for a maximum of 12 months. After that period, the Website will ask for the user's consent again. The user may revoke it sooner at any time through the means indicated in section 7.

9. Consequences of rejecting cookies

Rejecting analytics cookies does not affect the operation of the Website, which will remain fully available and functional. The only consequence is that no aggregated statistical information about your browsing will be collected.
